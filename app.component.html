How we work

Transparent, timeline-driven builds

  1. 1

    Understand & sketch

    We confirm size, colors, script (English/Devnagari), and any vastu or brand constraints.

  2. 2

    Material prep

    Wood is kiln-dried and leveled; pigments and metallic powders are sampled for approval.

  3. 3

    Pour & finish

    Resin pours in temperature-controlled batches; cured pieces are sanded, buffed, and sealed.

  4. 4

    Dispatch pan-India

    Foam-crated packaging with fragile handling; live WhatsApp updates till delivery.
