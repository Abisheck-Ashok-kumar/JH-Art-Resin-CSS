About J&H Resin Arts

Every piece is poured, polished and packed with love.

Our Story

J&H Resin Arts began by making custom gifts for friends and family - baby announcement frames, wedding memories and fun keychains. The appreciation turned that hobby into a growing brand.

Today, we create personalised resin frames, wooden frames, clocks, name stands and keychains for customers across India.

What We Believe In

  • Personalisation: Every design is built around your story, not a fixed template.
  • Quality: We choose good-grade resin, wood and embellishments for a long-lasting finish.
  • Transparency: We confirm design, price and timeline clearly before payment.
  • Support: We share care instructions and are reachable on WhatsApp.
